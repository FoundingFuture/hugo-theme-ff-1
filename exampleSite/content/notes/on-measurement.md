A short note, in a section whose own page carries no words. The theme
draws such a topic differently: it groups what is under it rather than
being somewhere you can go and read, so its name is set back from the
topics that have words of their own.

<!--more-->

Every number in this theme that describes the page was measured rather
than reasoned about. The band's height, the reading measure, the spacing
between two language codes. Each was wrong at least once, and each was
wrong in a way that looked right.
