A second note under the same wordless section, so the topic has more
than one thing to group.

<!--more-->

A default is a decision nobody remembers making. The ones that cost most
here were the defaults of other tools: Goldmark eats a backslash unless
told not to, and Chroma writes its colours into the markup unless told
to use classes. Both were silent, and both were wrong for weeks.
